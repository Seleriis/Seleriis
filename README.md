### 📦 Minecraft Projects

My projects are currently focused mainly on **Minecraft Forge 1.20.1** and compatibility between mods.

#### MCA Reborn x Easy Villagers Compat

A compatibility addon between **Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn** and **Easy Villagers**, allowing MCA villagers to be stored and restored without losing their identity, appearance, profession, family, relationships, and other important data.

- [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mca-reborn-x-easy-villagers-compat)
- [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/mca-reborn-x-easy-villagers-compat)

#### MCA Reborn x Vampirism Compat

A compatibility addon between **Minecraft Comes Alive Reborn** and **Vampirism**, with support for MCA vampire, hunter, and werewolf villagers while keeping them as real MCA entities.

- [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mca-reborn-x-vampirism-compat)
- [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/mca-reborn-x-vampirism-compat)
